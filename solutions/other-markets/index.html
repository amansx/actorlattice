Questions about another market

Do you already have a product for my industry?

The examples are opportunities to evaluate, not published ready-made vertical products. Bring one bounded process and the systems it depends on; the team can confirm whether the current product can support it.

Can we reuse the legal onboarding workflow unchanged?

The overall pattern of records, tasks and approvals may transfer, but each market needs its own data, policies, exceptions and acceptance tests.

Who makes the final regulated decision?

The responsible professional or organization retains that decision. The proposed automation can organize supporting information and handoffs within an agreed scope.
