Start with one process worth improving

ActorLattice starts with Brazilian law-firm onboarding and explores the same connected-work model across other markets.

Different businesses. Familiar handoffs.

Requests arrive. Documents are collected. Records change. Someone needs to approve the next step. When those handoffs depend on people remembering every detail, growth creates more coordination work.

ActorLattice's approach combines the records, rules and digital workers around a defined outcome.

How it works

Our first market: law firms

We are starting with Brazilian law firms and the work between a signed client and a matter ready for the team. Document requests, missing information, task assignments and billing handoffs create a concrete workflow to validate with practitioners.

Explore legal onboarding

Insurance intake, accounting document collection and internal service requests share parts of that pattern. Each needs its own records, controls and acceptance criteria; none should be treated as a legal workflow with its labels changed.

Explore other markets
