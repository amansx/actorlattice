Evidence starts with real work

Early adopters can help shape client onboarding for law firms around the work their teams actually do. We want to understand the current process, define a useful first improvement and learn from its results. We will not fill this page with unverified testimonials or projected savings.

Early-adopter questions

What would we agree before a pilot?

Choose the workflow, baseline, data boundary, human approvals, integrations and observable acceptance criteria. The scope should be small enough for the team doing the work to judge.

Will our firm's name appear on this site?

Only with the firm's explicit permission and a reviewed story that accurately describes observed results. Joining the early-adopter program is not a public customer endorsement.

What does success mean at this stage?

The team should see whether the scoped handoffs and exception handling work as agreed. Any measured time or effort result needs a source, baseline and measurement period before publication.
