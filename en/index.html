The parts work better together

A document arrives. Its status changes the matter record. That record tells an actor which task is next. Permissions determine what it may do. If interpretation is needed, AI assists; if a decision is needed, a person reviews it. Everyone works from the same process state.

Routine steps follow rules. Ambiguous steps ask for help.

Not every task needs an AI decision. Requesting a missing document or assigning a known next step can follow a defined rule. Interpreting a document may need AI assistance. Accepting an exception may need a person.

The aim is not maximum autonomy. It is the right level of delegation for each step, with clear boundaries around consequential actions.

Questions about getting started

Do we need to replace our firm's existing systems?

Start by identifying the system that owns each record. ActorLattice can be evaluated against one defined workflow and its handoffs. The actual integration scope depends on the firm's systems and the evaluated release.

Does AI make every decision?

No. Routine steps can follow rules, interpretation can use bounded AI assistance and consequential actions can require a person. Define those boundaries with the team before a pilot.

Is ActorLattice only for lawyers?

No. Brazilian law firms are the first market and onboarding is the first detailed workflow. The platform is designed for multiple markets, each with its own records, rules and evaluation.
