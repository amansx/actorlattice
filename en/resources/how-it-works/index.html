Guide · How ActorLattice works

Your business defines the outcome. ActorLattice organizes the work.

A plain-language guide to ActorLattice's business data, actors, rules, screens and use of AI.

Start with goals, rules and people

Describe what the business wants to accomplish. Define the information the process needs, the actions it permits and the people responsible for decisions. ActorLattice's design turns that operating model into a shared application and repeatable work.

Four parts inside the platform

Business data holds the records and status the process needs.

Actors are digital workers with defined responsibilities. They handle repeatable tasks within the authority they are given.

Rules and permissions determine what may happen and who must approve it.

Screens and forms give people a way to provide information, see progress and resolve exceptions.

An email, document system or billing service participates when a defined step needs it. The integration is part of the workflow, with an expected result and a clear failure boundary.

Routine steps can follow rules. Interpreting an unfamiliar document may require AI assistance. An ambiguous or consequential result can go to a person before work continues. The goal is controlled progress, not using AI for every action.

A client signs. The process requests the required documents, makes missing information visible, updates the matter and assigns work. The team reviews exceptions and approves the actions that need its judgment.

Questions about the operating model

What does a digital worker do?

It handles a defined task using only the information and actions assigned to it. For legal onboarding, that might be a completeness check or a prepared follow-up. A person reviews ambiguous or consequential decisions.

When does ActorLattice use an outside service?

Only at a defined workflow step that needs it, with the operation and expected result agreed for that integration. The exact service must be confirmed during evaluation.

What happens when a document is unclear?

The process should show the unresolved question to an authorized person and wait for their decision before dependent work continues.
